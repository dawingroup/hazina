# Valuation policy (template for the finance data lead)

Agree one method per asset class before sprint 2. Every value in Hazina shows its method, date and confidence.

| Asset class        | Method                                                       | Source                                              | Refresh       | Default confidence |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| Cash, money market | Market price                                                 | Statement                                           | Monthly       | High               |
| T-bills, bonds     | Cost plus accrued interest, or market price                  | Bank of Uganda auction results, custodian statement | Monthly       | High               |
| Listed shares      | Market price                                                 | USE closing price                                   | Monthly       | High               |
| Dawin subsidiaries | NAV or earnings multiple, per company                        | DawinOS monthly close                               | Quarterly     | Medium             |
| Land, property     | Professional valuation, or owner estimate between valuations | Valuer's report                                     | Every 2 years | Low to medium      |
| Vehicles           | Cost less straight-line depreciation                         | Purchase record                                     | Annually      | Medium             |
| SACCO shares       | Statement value                                              | SACCO statement                                     | Annually      | Medium             |

Open: which DawinOS figures are reliable enough today to value each subsidiary.
